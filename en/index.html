01Who we are
Who we are

Integrated technologies and sustainable engineering

TIES – Integrated Technologies and Sustainable Engineering is a research group affiliated with the Federal University of Itajubá (UNIFEI), within the Institutes of Mechanical Engineering (IEM) and Natural Resources (IRN). The group develops projects for Society, Industry and Academia in areas involving Energy, Integrated Systems, Smart Grids, Earthen Construction, Mechanical Metamaterials, Artificial Intelligence, Smart Construction, Structures and Numerical Methods, seeking sustainable and balanced solutions to challenges associated with societal development.

View the group profile at CNPq ↗
Group objective

To bring together materials technologies, renewable energy, generation systems and construction systems, integrating expertise to reduce impacts and improve the sustainability of engineering solutions.

Year established2021
