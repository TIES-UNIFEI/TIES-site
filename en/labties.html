Facilities · Digital manufacturing · Outreach

Laboratory of Integrated Technologies and Sustainable Engineering

LabTIES brings together resources and expertise to support research, teaching, outreach and technological development, connecting design, digital fabrication and prototyping.

3D scanning

Portable 3D scanner

Equipment for capturing the geometry of physical objects and supporting reverse engineering, documentation and prototype development.

3D printing · FDM

Creality K1 Max

Fused deposition modelling printer used to manufacture parts, models and prototypes for academic and research activities.

3D printing · Resin

Resin 3D printer

Photopolymerization resource for producing detailed models and parts. The specific model will be identified in a future update.

University outreach · Education

3D-printed educational materials

The laboratory’s outreach activities include developing and 3D-printing educational resources for classroom use, with applications in Biology and Sexual Education.
