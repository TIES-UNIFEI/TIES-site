Who we are

Integrated technologies and sustainable engineering

TIES – Integrated Technologies and Sustainable Engineering is a research group certified in the Brazilian National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq) Research Groups Directory. It is affiliated with the Federal University of Itajubá (UNIFEI), within the Institute of Mechanical Engineering. Established in 2021, the group develops projects that connect different fields of engineering and basic sciences, seeking sustainable and balanced solutions to challenges associated with societal development.

View the group profile at CNPq ↗
Group objective

To bring together numerical methods, materials technologies, renewable energy and construction-system technologies with knowledge from mathematics, chemistry and physics, integrating expertise to reduce impacts and improve the sustainability of engineering solutions.
